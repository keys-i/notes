The active gameplay effects use the supplied pacman_*.wav arcade samples.
No meow or ambient danger track is loaded by the site. The beginning and
intermission samples play once during the ready and power-up scenes. The
chomp sample runs only while collecting pellets; foreground cues interrupt
the optional music and finish before it resumes.

recovery-groove.wav is an original, locally synthesized 112 BPM instrumental
arcade loop: soft plucked lead, sine bass, kick, snare, and light hi-hats.
It contains no sampled recordings or vocals. Background music is off by
default and is available only through the separate Music control.

recovery-tick.wav and recovery-boom.wav are original, locally synthesized
one-shot effects for the 5–1 countdown and BOOM respectively. The countdown
combines a bass strike, metallic echoes, and a rising electronic swell; each
successive hit increases in pitch and gain. The 1.35-second impact combines a falling
bass tone, low harmonics, a noise crack, and short reflections. Neither contains sampled recordings, vocals,
or a music loop.
