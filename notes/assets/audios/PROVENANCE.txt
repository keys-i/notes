The meow_*.wav files are locally rendered cartoon meow performances derived
from the supplied arcade references. The two looping music tracks keep the
recognisable retro cue in front and add a low-passed meow vocal layer; the
short gameplay cues remain meow-led.

meow_launch.wav adds a low-frequency rocket transient to the extra-life meow
for the final countdown hit.

The supplied pacman_*.wav reference files are development inputs and are not
loaded by the site.
