The meow_*.wav files are locally rendered cartoon meow performances mixed
with a quiet copy of the supplied arcade reference. That keeps the original
note timing and melody recognisable while the meow remains the lead voice.

meow_launch.wav adds a low-frequency rocket transient to the extra-life meow
for the final countdown hit.

The supplied pacman_*.wav reference files are development inputs and are not
loaded by the site.
