# Tutorial 7 — Relational Algebra

Use:

- $STUDENT(sid,name,major)$
- $ENROLMENT(sid,cid,mark)$
- $COURSE(cid,title)$

## Build from the output backwards

For “names of IT students enrolled in Databases”:

1. the output needs `name`, so finish with $\pi_{name}$
2. `name` and `major` come from `STUDENT`
3. course title comes from `COURSE`
4. `ENROLMENT` connects the two keys
5. push each selection to the relation that owns its attributes

$$
\pi_{name}
\left(
\sigma_{major='IT'}(STUDENT)
\bowtie ENROLMENT
\bowtie
\sigma_{title='Databases'}(COURSE)
\right)
$$

The natural joins are safe here only because the shared attribute names are
the intended join keys

## Translate “none”

Students with no enrolment can be expressed as:

$$
\pi_{sid}(STUDENT)-\pi_{sid}(ENROLMENT)
$$

## Translate “all”

Students enrolled in every course from $REQUIRED(cid)$:

$$
\pi_{sid,cid}(ENROLMENT)\div REQUIRED
$$

## Check the expression

- every referenced attribute exists at that point in the tree
- set operators receive union-compatible inputs
- projections do not discard a later join attribute
- joins have explicit conditions unless a natural join is intentionally safe
- the final schema exactly matches the requested output
