# Tutorial 9 — Query Optimisation

## Estimate before rewriting

For 100,000 rows and 500 uniformly distributed department values:

$$
sel(department=v)=\frac{1}{500}=0.002
$$

$$
|result|\approx100000\times0.002=200
$$

If 30 per cent of rows are active and the predicates are independent:

$$
sel(department=v\land active)=0.002\times0.3=0.0006
$$

Correlation can make this estimate wrong, so distinguish a model estimate from
an observed count

## Rewrite a tree

Starting from a product of `STUDENT`, `ENROLMENT` and `COURSE` followed by one
large selection:

1. split `AND` conditions
2. push single-relation predicates to their leaves
3. turn key-equality product conditions into joins
4. join the smallest filtered inputs first
5. project unused attributes after preserving required join keys

## Validate the rewrite

- outer-join null-preservation has not changed
- duplicate semantics still match the SQL query
- a projection has not removed an attribute needed above
- volatile expressions are not evaluated a different number of times
- constraints used for simplification actually exist

Heuristics narrow the search space, while cost estimates choose among the
remaining physical plans
