# Week 10 — Numerical Solution of the Heat Equation

## Governing equation

$$
\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}
=D\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x^2}
$$

On a uniform grid define

$$
\sigma=D\frac{\Delta t}{\Delta x^2}
$$

## Explicit update

$$
u_i^{n+1}
=
\sigma u_{i-1}^{n}
+(1-2\sigma)u_i^n
+\sigma u_{i+1}^n
$$

The one-dimensional stability requirement is

$$
0\le\sigma\le\frac12
$$

```python
for _ in range(time_steps):
    updated = values.copy()
    updated[1:-1] = (
        sigma * values[:-2]
        + (1 - 2 * sigma) * values[1:-1]
        + sigma * values[2:]
    )
    updated[0] = left_boundary
    updated[-1] = right_boundary
    values = updated
```

## Implicit update

Backward Euler gives

$$
-\sigma u_{i-1}^{n+1}
+(1+2\sigma)u_i^{n+1}
-\sigma u_{i+1}^{n+1}
=u_i^n
$$

Build the tridiagonal matrix once and reuse its factorisation at each time step

## Checks

- apply boundary values at the new time level
- keep old and new arrays separate for the explicit method
- verify $\sigma$ before stepping
- halve $\Delta x$ and reduce $\Delta t$ consistently
- compare total heat and extrema with the boundary conditions
