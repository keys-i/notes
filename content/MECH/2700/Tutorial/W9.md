# Week 9 — Bicycle Frame Optimisation

## Reduced-Order Model

`LBM_opt.npz` contains Lattice–Boltzmann pressure histories for candidate frame
cross-sectional areas. `lbm_demo.py` loads and inspects the data

For each area $a$, reduce the pressure history to its mean-square fluctuation

$$
\bar p(a)=\frac{1}{T}\int_0^T p(t;a)\,dt
$$

$$
\hat p(a)=\frac{1}{T}\int_0^T
\left[p(t;a)-\bar p(a)\right]^2\,dt
$$

Smaller area reduces fabrication cost, while larger area reduces the pressure
fluctuations. The combined objective is

$$
C(a)=10^5\hat p(a)+\left(\frac{a}{3}\right)^2
$$

## General-Basis Least Squares

Fit the sampled fluctuation values with a basis chosen for the observed trend

$$
\hat p(a)\approx
c_0\phi_0(a)+c_1\phi_1(a)+\cdots+c_m\phi_m(a)
$$

For sample areas $a_i$, form

$$
A_{ij}=\phi_j(a_i),
\qquad
\boldsymbol c
=\underset{\boldsymbol c}{\operatorname{argmin}}
\|A\boldsymbol c-\boldsymbol{\hat p}\|_2
$$

```python
import numpy as np


def fit_general_basis(area, fluctuation, basis):
    design = np.column_stack([function(area) for function in basis])
    coefficients, *_ = np.linalg.lstsq(design, fluctuation, rcond=None)
    return coefficients


def evaluate_basis(area, coefficients, basis):
    return sum(
        coefficient * function(area)
        for coefficient, function in zip(coefficients, basis, strict=True)
    )
```

Plot the fitted curve over the sample points before using it in the objective

## Golden-Section Search

With the fitted fluctuation model $\hat p_{\mathrm{fit}}(a)$, minimise

$$
C_{\mathrm{fit}}(a)
=10^5\hat p_{\mathrm{fit}}(a)+\left(\frac{a}{3}\right)^2
$$

over a bracket contained within the sampled area range. Golden-section search
reuses one interior objective value at each iteration and contracts the bracket
by

$$
\rho=\frac{\sqrt5-1}{2}
$$

Stop when the bracket width is below the required area tolerance

## Checks

- integrate over the complete pressure record when computing $\bar p$ and
  $\hat p$
- keep area units consistent in both terms of $C(a)$
- do not optimise outside the range supported by `LBM_opt.npz`
- verify that the fitted objective is unimodal inside the chosen bracket
- compare the optimum cost with both bracket endpoints
