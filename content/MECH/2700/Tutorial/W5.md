# Week 5 — Crane Truss Structure

## Unknown member forces

Assign one unknown force to every truss member and support reaction

Use a consistent sign convention

- tension acts away from a joint
- compression appears as a negative solved force
- horizontal and vertical reactions are separate unknowns

## Joint equilibrium

For each joint

$$
\sum F_x=0,
\qquad
\sum F_y=0
$$

A member joining nodes $a$ and $b$ has direction

$$
\hat{\mathbf e}_{ab}
=
\frac{(x_b-x_a,\ y_b-y_a)}
{\sqrt{(x_b-x_a)^2+(y_b-y_a)^2}}
$$

Insert its $x$ and $y$ components into the matching rows of

$$
A\mathbf f=\mathbf b
$$

## Assembly pattern

```python
for member_index, (node_a, node_b) in enumerate(members):
    delta = coordinates[node_b] - coordinates[node_a]
    direction = delta / np.linalg.norm(delta)

    A[2 * node_a : 2 * node_a + 2, member_index] += direction
    A[2 * node_b : 2 * node_b + 2, member_index] -= direction
```

Place applied loads on the right-hand side with the opposite sign

## Solve

Use pivoted elimination or LU factorisation

```python
forces = np.linalg.solve(A, b)
residual = A @ forces - b
```

## Checks

- the matrix has one row per equilibrium equation and one column per unknown
- every member contributes equal and opposite directions at its two joints
- $\lVert A\mathbf f-\mathbf b\rVert_\infty$ is small
- support reactions balance the total external load
- label each negative member force as compression
